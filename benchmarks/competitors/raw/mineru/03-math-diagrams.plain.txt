Math, Diagrams and Charts

Math and diagram fences are rendered in the browser, at print time: KaTeX typesets the math, Mermaid draws the diagrams, Chart.js paints the charts. All three settle under one window.__carveReady promise that print_cdp.py awaits, so nothing is half-drawn when the page is captured. Without the corresponding library installed, each construct degrades to its readable source

Inline and display math

The mass-energy relation E = m c ^ { 2 } appears inline in a sentence, while the Gaussian integral is shown as a centered display equation:
